## Full-view comparison

- The previous Android `AlertDialog` used a narrow text summary and did not reproduce the iOS poster. It has been replaced with a full-screen share flow and a 360 dp poster.
- The final screen matches the iOS information hierarchy: transparent overlay toolbar, cover hero, eyebrow and scope badge, large title and destination/date metadata, quote summary, nested day panels, D-number markers, count badges, item cards, completion state, time pills, and the two share exits below the poster.
- Android range selection remains available as a dedicated bottom sheet, supporting one day, multiple days, and the entire trip or footprint without crowding the poster.
- Real `MediaReference` images are decoded for both the cover and a one/two-column item-photo grid. The Android verification dataset has no stored media, so the accepted screenshot intentionally shows the designed no-cover decoration; this is a dataset difference rather than a missing image surface.

## Required fidelity surfaces

- Fonts and typography: Android keeps the native Noto/Roboto Chinese stack, with increased optical weight and contrast for title, section title, item title, metadata, and time labels. The hierarchy and wrapping now correspond to the iOS card instead of the old faint dialog text.
- Spacing and layout rhythm: the poster is 360 dp wide with a 252 dp cover, 20 dp paper padding, 18 dp section rhythm, 20 dp day radius, 16 dp item radius, 36 dp day markers, and compact 25 dp item-state markers. At the matched scroll position the persistent toolbar overlays the cover like iOS.
- Colors and visual tokens: warm paper gradient, pale green summary, sage day panels, off-white item cards, teal rail, teal badges, completed green, and dark ink text are mapped directly from `ShareExportView.swift` values.
- Image quality and asset fidelity: stored images use center-crop in the live preview and exported PNG, respect the footprint cover zoom/offset, and are emitted at 3x poster density. Missing-media state uses the same restrained circle/map decoration concept as iOS instead of a blank block.
- Copy and content: `分享预览`, `完成`, scope names, `个片段`, `分享精美长图`, and `发送可导入的…` follow the iOS terminology. Trip and footprint content map title, dates, summary, day narrative, item detail, automatic status, and media rather than flattening them into bullet strings.
- Accessibility boundary: icon buttons retain descriptions, primary controls use practical touch targets, and visible contrast is materially improved. TalkBack traversal, large-font reflow, switch access, and reduced-motion behavior remain separate device checks and are not claimed from screenshot QA.

## Comparison history

- Iteration 1 P1: Android used a small generic `AlertDialog`; the iOS source is a full-screen, high-detail poster experience. Replaced the dialog with a full-screen Compose flow.
- Iteration 1 P1: preview and generated PNG flattened items into bullet strings and omitted summary, narratives, status, details, and media. Added structured preview models and implemented all poster regions in both renderers.
- Iteration 1 P1: no cover or item-photo rendering. Added real local-media decode, cover crop/zoom/offset, and up-to-four-photo grids.
- Iteration 2 P2: the Android toolbar occupied a separate opaque band, preventing the iOS overlay composition at the matched scroll position. Made it transparent, allowed content to scroll underneath, and switched toolbar foreground color over the poster.
- Iteration 2 P2: the no-media test state looked too empty. Added the iOS-derived restrained cover decoration while keeping real media as the primary path.
- Post-fix evidence: `/private/tmp/triptrail-share-design-comparison-final.png` shows no remaining actionable P0/P1/P2 structural, hierarchy, spacing, color, typography, or interaction mismatch. The visible photo and copy-density differences come from the two simulators' different local datasets.

## Accepted P3 differences

- Android and iOS retain their native system status/navigation bars, font rasterizers, and icon families.
- The iOS reference contains a real cover, longer narrative, and four photos; the Android test footprint has no media and shorter text, so dynamic card heights differ.
- Exact Chinese glyph metrics differ between platform system fonts while weight, contrast, and hierarchy remain aligned.
